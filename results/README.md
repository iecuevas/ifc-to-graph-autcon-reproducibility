# Results included in this package

- `timings_summary.csv`: aggregate timing statistics reported in the revised manuscript (five runs per model) plus the published IFC-Graph baseline time and descriptive ratio.
- `graph_counts.csv`: published baseline and proposed node/relationship counts reported in the revised manuscript.

The published baseline comes from Zhu, Wu, and Lei (2023), DOI `10.1016/j.autcon.2023.104778`.

## Raw runs

The 40 individual timing observations are **not** included because they were not present in the supplied source ZIP and were not recoverable by content search of the archive. They must not be reconstructed from aggregate statistics. If authentic raw records are later recovered, they should be added as a separate file and checked by recomputing mean, SD, and CV.
